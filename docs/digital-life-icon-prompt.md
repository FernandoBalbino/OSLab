## Prompt final

Create one distinctive Windows desktop application icon for a Portuguese educational course named 'Minha Primeira Vida Digital'. No text or lettering. A polished Fluent-style 3D icon: a bold blue-violet personal identity card with a large white person silhouette, accompanied by a bright teal envelope and a small warm coral play button. Three simple, chunky forms, immediately recognizable even at 48 pixels. The identity card is the dominant shape, envelope in the lower left and play tile in the lower right. Clean professional app icon, soft rounded corners, subtle luminous highlights, restrained dimensional shading, balanced centered composition, no globe, no browser window, no logo from any company, no decorative particles, no small details. Objects occupy 85 percent of the square canvas. Transparent background outside the icon, with natural soft object shadow only.
